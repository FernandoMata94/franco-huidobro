Education

02 / Background

June 2025

MSc in Science of Engineering — Power Electronics and Control Systems

Tecnológico de Monterrey, Monterrey, Mexico

Master's thesis: A Controller for an LCI to Extend the Operating Range of Single-Phase Brushless Exciter Synchronous Motors. Developed an advanced control strategy for load-commutated inverter (LCI) drives for SAG mills, introducing a novel flux-based load-synchronized pulse scheme and real-time inverter firing angle adjustment. Validated the system using hardware-in-the-loop with dSPACE hardware (HIL) simulations under realistic industrial milling profiles, successfully extending the motor's speed and load operating range. Published in the Tecnológico de Monterrey Institutional Repository (RITEC).

Supplementary Analysis: Developed a high-fidelity mathematical model to evaluate wireless power transfer (WPT) as an alternative excitation method for synchronous motors. Analyzed system behavior at low-speed/standstill conditions to propose efficiency improvements over traditional inductive coupling
